## 📌 LEARNING RULES

For every topic, follow this learning sequence:

1. Understand the concept.
2. Learn the terminology.
3. Understand the syntax.
4. Study practical examples.
5. Write code independently.
6. Observe browser output.
7. Complete exercises.
8. Take a short quiz.
9. Revise previous concepts.
10. Move to the next topic only after understanding.

**Final Goal:** Become confident in writing HTML independently, understanding website structure, and building real-world web pages from scratch.
